What it is

Star Gazing Night is a fictional evening for anyone who wants to look at the stars. No telescope needed, beginners welcome.

What to bring

Bring a blanket, a warm jacket, and a flashlight with red light if you have one. Let your eyes get used to the dark.

How I tested it
